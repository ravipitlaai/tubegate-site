# Sponsor TubeGate

TubeGate is a free Chrome extension that helps parents choose which YouTube videos can play. It shows one small,
labelled sponsored suggestion to the adult who manages it: in its admin console and, on the Adult profile, in its
popup and on its own block screen. Children never see ads.

## What we accept

Products and services that help families learn, create and stay well, for example tutoring and online courses,
learning apps and toys, books and reading, STEM and coding, language learning, music lessons, art and crafts, family
fitness, mindfulness, careers and skills, productivity tools, family and parenting services, outdoors and travel,
charities, and software.

## What we never accept

Gambling, betting or lotteries; alcohol, tobacco or vaping; drugs or cannabis; adult content or dating; weapons;
crypto, trading, loans or get-rich schemes; diet pills, weight loss or cosmetic surgery; unproven supplements or
miracle cures; horror or violent games; political or religious campaigning; junk food; prize draws and "you have won"
offers; anything that imitates a system warning.

## How ads work

- Text ad: sponsor name, title (up to 90 characters), one sentence (up to 220), a button label and an https link to
  your own site. Link shorteners are not accepted.
- Ads are matched to what the family uses TubeGate for (Learning, Fitness, Calm and so on), never to what anyone
  watched. No personal data is shared with sponsors.
- No tracking pixels. Measure results with UTM tags on your link.
- Affiliate links are accepted and always labelled "Affiliate link".
- Every ad is reviewed by hand before it goes live.
